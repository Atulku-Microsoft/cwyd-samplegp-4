# Sample Questions

To help you get started, here are some **Sample Prompts** you can ask in the app:

### **Chat with Your Data**
Easily interact with your data using natural language to get instant, AI-powered answers and insights from your documents.
![cwyd-sampledata](images/cwyd-sampledata.png)
_Sample Questions:_

- How do I enroll in health benefits as a new employee?
- What options are available to me in terms of health coverage?
- What providers are available under each option?
- Can I access my current provider?
- What benefits are available to employees (besides health coverage)?
- How do I enroll in employee benefits?
- Task: Switch to other chat history tab to show next questions
- Can I extend my benefits to cover my spouse or dependents?
  
The Chat with Your Data Solution Accelerator lets users interact with their data through natural language queries. It provides instant, AI-driven answers and insights from uploaded or connected documents.
